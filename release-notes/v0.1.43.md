## New
- **Clickable links and email addresses:** web addresses in messages are now links, including ones written without "https://" like tvtracker.online, and so are email addresses. Clicking an email address starts a new message to it in Coo.
- **Links as you write:** a web or email address you type or paste into a message becomes a link. ⌘-click a link in the editor to open it.
- **Coo as your email app:** make Coo the Mac's default email app in Settings › General or in the welcome tour, and email links in other apps and on the web open a new message in Coo.

## Improved
- The formatting buttons' tooltips show their keyboard shortcuts, so the RTL/LTR hint under the editor is gone.

## Fixed
- Coo now checks for updates whenever you come back to it, including from the menu bar or a compose window. Before, it only checked when the main window got focus.
- 0.1.42 showed its version as 0.1.41 in About and Settings.
