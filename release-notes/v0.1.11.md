## New
- **Search suggestions:** start typing an operator (from:, to:, in:, is:, has:, account:, before:, after:) and Coo suggests it, then its values: contacts for from: and to:, your folders for in:, your accounts, and quick dates. An empty search box shows examples to try.
- **Default calendar for new events:** choose it in Settings › Calendar, or right-click a calendar (or its account) in the calendar sidebar. Read-only calendars like holidays can't be picked.
- **All-day events:** click an empty spot in a day's all-day row to create one.
- **Menus:** shortcuts now line up, and every shortcut has a menu item: Mail and Calendar (⌘1/⌘2), the command palette, next/previous conversation, Label, Undo Last Action, and a new Calendar menu with New Event and Join Current Meeting. Toggle Full Screen moved to the Window menu.
- **Save Image…** when you right-click an image in a message. Items that did nothing (Open in New Window, Download Image, Reload) are gone from that menu.

## Composer
- A new line starts in the direction of the line above it (right-to-left after Hebrew), until you type a letter.
- Clicking anywhere in the message area puts the cursor in the text, at the nearest spot.
- Inline code and code blocks are readable in dark mode, and stay readable for recipients reading in dark mode.

## Notifications
- A burst of new mail across accounts plays one sound, not one per account.
- No sounds while the screen is off or locked (banners still show).
