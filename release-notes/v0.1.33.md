## New
- **High contrast:** one switch in Settings › Appearance (and in the welcome tour) turns whichever theme you use into its strongest version: black or white text, solid edges, accents and grey text you can read at a glance, and a clearer focus ring. Works with every theme, light and dark.

## Improved
- **Theme changes glide:** switching theme, light or dark, or high contrast now fades the colors over instead of snapping.
- **Search and folders:** clicking a mailbox or folder in the sidebar closes search and takes you there.
