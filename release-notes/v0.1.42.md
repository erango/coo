## Improved
- **A better Label picker:** labels already on the conversation are ticked at the top, followed by recent ones, then everything else with nested labels under their parent. Tick and untick several, see what will change, and Apply. Matching letters light up as you type, and Enter still labels the highlighted one in a single step. Gmail's own star labels no longer clutter the list.
- **Move to** has the same cleaner look.

## Fixed
- Replies an AI agent drafted and sent could leave a "Draft" label in the list with no draft to open. These leftovers are now cleared, and a reply draft saved in Coo opens when you open its conversation.
- The focus ring of the "Quoted" button was cut off at its left edge.
- Hebrew label and folder names in the sidebar now line up at the left like the others.
