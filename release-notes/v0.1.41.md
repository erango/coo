## New
- **Watches and Needs you:** tell Coo what mail you care about (a sender, a subject, a conversation) and it catches it for you in Needs you, under the Inbox, with a notification if you like. Add a note for your assistant ("check Friday orders"), and Claude can pick these up when you ask what needs you, even Claude Desktop. Set them up in Settings › Watches, or right-click a conversation and choose Watch this conversation.
- **AI agents can watch for new mail:** an agent can ask Coo to tell it when mail arrives on a conversation, from someone, or with a subject, and keep an eye on it in the background (`coo watch`). Agents can list and stop their watches.
- **Update in the morning hello:** when an update is waiting, the day's first greeting says so and offers Restart to update.
