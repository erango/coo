## New
- **Main address:** pick the alias an account goes by in Settings › Accounts (**Make main**). The sidebar shows it, and new messages from that account come from it. Works for iCloud aliases (including your own domain) and Gmail or Microsoft send-as addresses.

## Improved
- **Garbled Hebrew:** when a lost א turned into a space, Coo no longer splits the word in two ("מאושר", not "מם ושר"; "האם המאמר", not "הם ם המם מר"), including where the plain-text version wrapped the line there. When both letters make a real word, Coo picks the likelier one ("אנא", not "אנם"; "נושא:", not "נושם:").
- Losing the network (sleep, switching Wi-Fi) no longer files sync errors in crash reports, and crash reports no longer include calendar names.
