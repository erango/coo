## New
- **Archive after replying or forwarding:** Settings › Writing can archive a conversation once your reply or forward is sent. It's off by default; your first reply (and first forward) from Coo celebrates and asks whether you'd like it.

## Improved
- **AI agents show up right away:** what an agent does through Coo (sending a reply, archiving, saving a draft) appears in the list and in the open conversation within a second or two, instead of on the next sync.
- **The open conversation stays current:** a reply that arrives while you're reading shows up in place.
- **Agents stay connected through updates:** after Coo updates, an agent's connection restarts by itself; the "Reconnect your AI agent" steps only appear if it really can't.

## Fixed
- A draft an agent sent could come back as a local copy, leaving a "Draft" label on the conversation with nothing in Drafts.
