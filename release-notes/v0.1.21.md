## New
- **Keyboard shortcut styles:** Gmail (R, A, F, E…) or Outlook (⌘R, ⇧⌘R, ⌘J, ⌃E…), in Settings › Keyboard shortcuts. Tooltips, menus and hints show the keys of the style you pick.
- **Find in a conversation:** ⌘F with a conversation open finds text in it; ↵ and ⇧↵ step through the matches, Esc closes. With nothing open, ⌘F still searches the folder.
- **Gmail labels:** create one from the Labels group (the + on hover, or right-click), delete one from its right-click menu. Dragging messages onto a label now adds it, and they stay where they are.
- **⌘ + scroll** changes the text size, with a mouse wheel or a trackpad.

## Improved
- A text size change applies to every open window at once, message windows and composers included.

## Fixed
- Shortcuts (R, A, F, E…) work after clicking into a message.
- Sending no longer leaves extra copies of the draft behind in Drafts.
