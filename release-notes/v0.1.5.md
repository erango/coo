## New
- **Attach from Google Drive:** pick files in Google's own picker, then attach a copy or insert a link. Google Docs, Sheets and Slides attach as PDF or Office files, your choice. Coo only gets access to the files you pick, and copies are fetched as the message sends, never saved on your Mac.
- **Account nicknames:** click an account's name in Settings › Accounts to call it "Work" or "Home" everywhere in Coo.
- **A friendlier reading pane** when nothing is open: the bird waits for you to pick a conversation.

## Fixed
- Long messages open at full height right away, and no longer keep growing while you scroll.
- Notifications with long subjects show their full text.
