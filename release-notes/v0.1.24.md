## New
- **Reuse message:** send a message again with small changes. It opens a new message to the same people with the same subject, its text and files ready to edit (right-click a message, or the button next to Forward).
- **Hover actions:** choose up to 5 buttons a message row shows under the pointer, in your order, from anything in its right-click menu (Settings › Appearance; right-click a row's buttons to get there).

## Fixed
- Messages showed no content in 0.1.23 (only the preview in the list), and keyboard shortcuts and links stopped working. Both work again.
- Some newsletters made the message grow taller and taller, with the content pushed to the bottom. Messages now fit their content, and shrink when the window widens.
- Emails attached by dragging them onto a message arrive named after their subject (.eml), not "part-1".
