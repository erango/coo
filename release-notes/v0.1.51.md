## New
- Conversations open in the message list. Click a conversation's count to see its messages under it, newest first, and click one to read it alone. Settings › Reading › Conversations switches back to one row per conversation.
- Select several attachments: tick the circle on each, ⌘-click or Shift-click them, or drag a box around them. Then save or open them together, or drag them all out to a folder.
- In a conversation, click a message's header to fold it to a row, the newest one too.
- "Mark unread from here": the envelope button on a message in a conversation marks it and the newer ones unread.

## Improved
- In an inline reply, the quoted message shows at full length; the reply scrolls as one sheet and Send stays at the bottom.
- The formatting bar works on the quoted text of a reply or forward.
