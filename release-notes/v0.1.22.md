## New
- **Empty Trash:** right-click a Trash folder, or use Empty beside the search in Trash (Gmail empties its own Trash after 30 days).
- **Save messages as .eml:** right-click › Save as .eml…, or Message › Save As…; several at once go to a folder.
- **Attach emails:** drag messages from the list onto the message you're writing; they go along as .eml.
- **Drafts ask before saving** (Settings › Composing › Drafts), or save as you write and show when they last saved.
- **Mark as read after a few seconds** (Settings › General), instead of as soon as a message opens.
- **Composer attachments:** click for Quick Look, double-click to open, right-click to show in Finder.
- **Replies:** Cc and Bcc, a pop-out to their own window, and a reply you're writing follows the conversation into its own window and back.
- **Drag addresses** between To, Cc and Bcc.
- **Gmail labels:** opening a label also loads its older mail.
- Notification sounds: a "Send a test notification" button in Settings › Notifications.

## Improved
- Unread messages have a faintly tinted background, so they stand out at a glance.
- New messages come from the account whose folder you're in.
- Undo is ⌘Z and still works after its notice is gone.
- "Syncing…" shows only when you asked for a sync.
- Search suggestions find a person's address in any field.
- In Trash, no archive, delete, flag or read buttons; with attachments above, composers show them in their own strip.
- Messages view: no "Part of a conversation" link.

## Fixed
- Deleting a Gmail label works (it shows "Deleting…" meanwhile); New label only on the Labels group.
- Notification sounds play your chosen sound on recent macOS.
- An opened draft closed unchanged isn't saved again; empty messages never are.
- Opening a Microsoft draft that changed elsewhere no longer shows "Something went wrong".
