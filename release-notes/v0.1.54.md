## New
- Links in the message you're writing look like links: a hand pointer and a soft highlight on hover. Clicking one opens a small menu with its address and Open, Edit, Copy and Remove. ⌘-click still opens it straight away.
- Editing a link changes its text as well as its address.

## Fixed
- A web or email address typed at the very end of a message now goes out as a link. Any bare address left in a message is linked when it's sent or saved.
