## New
- **Welcome tour:** a short, friendly walk through the choices that make Coo yours: theme, how the inbox looks, threads or single messages, sender photos, Gmail-style or ⌘ keys, new-mail alerts, and reading. Every choice applies as you make it, and the pigeon hops along with you. Finish it and the pigeon delivers your first letter. It opens once on a new install; run it again any time from the Coo menu (Welcome Tour…), Settings › General, or ⌘K.

## Improved
- **Threads or messages** is now a setting (Settings › Appearance › Group mail) and Coo remembers it; the switch above the list is gone.
- **Replying:** a lighter, shorter fade above the reply box, and the box has a clear edge all round.
- **Senders' colors button** only shows where it changes something (dark mode, or a tinted light theme).
