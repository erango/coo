## Improved
- **Signing in again:** when accounts need to sign in again, each gets its own line in a bar above the message list instead of one card covering your mail. Signing in shows that it's waiting for your browser (with Cancel), and your mail stays readable meanwhile, handy when the sign-in emails you a code. The status line counts the accounts, names them all in its tooltip, and takes you to them when clicked. As soon as a sign-in succeeds, its warning goes away.
- **Calendar sidebar:** a long list of calendars now scrolls between the month and the footer, so the footer buttons stay in reach, with a soft shadow on whichever edge has more to see.
- **Quieter flags:** flagging and unflagging no longer show a notification.
- **Row buttons come alive:** the hover bar's flag waves (or lowers) right on the button you clicked, and the Mark read / unread envelope folds its flap open or shut.
