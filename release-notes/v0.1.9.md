## New
- **Default account for new messages:** choose it in Settings › Accounts (or Composing), or right-click an account in the sidebar and pick "Set as default for new messages". Without a choice, new messages come from the top account in the sidebar. Replies still come from the account the message was sent to.
- **Account menu:** right-click an account in the sidebar for New message, Set as default, Mute notifications, Copy address and Account settings.
- **Video calls for new events:** Settings › Calendar › Video call adds Google Meet or Zoom to every new event, optionally only when the event has no location. The link is made when you save, and you can change it per event.
- **Tooltips** on icon buttons show what they do and their shortcut, like "Archive (E)".
- **Settings slides in and out** instead of switching abruptly.

## Fixed
- A message list that's narrow, or a long folder name, no longer pushes Threads / Messages and New message out of view; the title shortens instead.
- Hovering a message no longer shows Archive as if it were pressed.
- Undo toasts show the icon of what you did (delete, move, flag…), not always the archive icon.
