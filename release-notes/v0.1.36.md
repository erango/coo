## New
- **Paste to link:** select text in a message you're writing and paste a web address or an email address, and the text becomes a link instead of being replaced.

## Improved
- **AI agents tell you when they need you:** if Coo turns an agent away (usually because Coo updated while the agent was running) or an agent needs an account whose sign-in expired, Coo now says so, with a notification when it's in the background, and the agent gets plain steps to pass on.
- **Archive is ⌘E** in the Outlook-style shortcuts (it was ⌃E).
- **Message list polish:** the list's header casts a soft shadow when you scroll, Cards and Day bands have even space on both sides, and the open message's outline is crisp and even on non-Retina displays.
