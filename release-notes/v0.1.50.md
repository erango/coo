## New
- VIPs and muted senders. Each account can notify you about all mail, only mail from VIPs, or nothing. Mark a person or a whole domain as a VIP or muted in Settings › Notifications, from the sender's card, or by right-clicking a message. VIPs get a star in the list and in their notification.
- Mentions. Type @ in a message to tag someone from its To or Cc. In mail you read, hover a mention to see who it is; click it to write to them.
- Pasting formatted text now matches your message: its font, size and paragraph direction, keeping bold, italic, underline, links and lists. A small bar after the paste switches to the original look or plain text, and Settings › Writing sets the default.

## Fixed
- A new message's window can be moved by its title strip, also to another screen.
- At bigger text sizes, an inline reply no longer runs past the bottom of the window, so the quoted message scrolls and Send stays in reach.
- The From menu in a reply closes after you pick an address, and when you click elsewhere or press Esc.
