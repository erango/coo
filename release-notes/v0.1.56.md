## New
- Meetings are much harder to miss. As one gets close, Coo gets louder, one step at a time:
  - The meeting and the minutes left show next to Coo's icon in the menu bar, and the Next up card warms up, then turns solid when it starts.
  - The reminder notification, as before.
  - A couple of minutes before, a banner across the top of Coo with Join, In 1 min and Not going. While you're writing, a strip in the message offers Save draft & join.
  - When it starts, a panel over every app (even full-screen ones) with Join and Dismiss. Put it away and it comes back once.
- Join, Dismiss or Not going stops the rest for that meeting.
- ⇧⌘J joins the meeting that's on from any app. The shortcut shows on the Join buttons.
- Every step is a setting in Settings › Notifications, with Gentle, Standard and Unmissable to set them all at once. You can also pick which meetings alert (tentative ones, events with just you) and calendars that stay quiet, and turn on a sound when a meeting starts.
