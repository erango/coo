## New
- **Separate sounds:** Settings › Notifications › Sounds turns the new-mail sound and the meeting-reminder sound on and off independently, so mail can be quiet while meetings still chime.

## Improved
- **Sync now:** the sync status at the bottom of the sidebar is a button: hover it to see "Sync now", click to sync.
- Coo checks for updates every 10 minutes at most when you come back to it, instead of hourly.

## Fixed
- **Dark mode, Cards and Day bands:** the message list sits a shade darker than the reading pane, so the two no longer run together, and the line between them runs the full height.
