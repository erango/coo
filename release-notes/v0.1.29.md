## New
- **Lines per message:** Settings › Appearance › Message list now lets you choose 3 lines per message (sender, subject, preview) or 2 (the preview follows the subject), so more messages fit when you want them to.

## Improved
- **A roomier message list:** rows have even space above and below their text in every list style, so unread rows no longer feel cramped against the next one, and sender photos sit centred in the row. Day bands now follow the lines setting too (pick 2 lines for their previous look).
