## A fresh look
- **Paper on stone:** the sidebar sits on a soft stone ground, and the message list and reader share one raised sheet. Unread messages get a faint tint as well as the dot and bold text; the open one a firmer tint and ring. Works in every theme, light and dark.
- **A quieter sidebar:** accounts are one line each with their unread count (click to open their folders). Mail, Calendar, Sync, appearance and Settings live in a small bar at the bottom, and a status line slides up only when there's news.
- **Classic list** rows now run edge to edge between hairlines.
- **Settings, redesigned:** nine clearer pages (General, Appearance, Accounts & agents, Reading, Writing, Calendar, Notifications, Privacy, Keyboard shortcuts), with previews of every theme and list style, your AI agents and their status right on the page, and a bell per account to mute its notifications.
- **New themes:** Mint (our own, minty fresh) and Cyberdream (neon), each in light and dark, replace Catppuccin and Gruvbox. If you used one of those, you're moved over automatically.

## New
- **Empty Trash in Gmail:** Gmail accounts can now empty Trash and delete messages in Trash for good. Coo asks Google for full access to Gmail; accounts you added before are asked once, in your browser, the first time.
- **Select by dragging:** drag from empty space in the message list to select the messages a rectangle covers (⌘ or ⇧ adds to the selection).
- **Sender photos from your contacts:** photos of people in your Google contacts, and in your Outlook contacts and organization, when you allow it in Settings › Appearance.
- **Your own shortcuts:** actions without a key in your shortcut style (Reuse message, Open in a new window, Save as .eml…, Report phishing and more) can take one of yours, checked against Coo's keys and macOS's.
- **⌘F follows focus:** in an open message it finds in the message; after a click in the list it searches the folder.
- **AI agents** can delete for good and add a Google Meet or Zoom call to events they create.
- **Report a Bug…** is also in the Coo menu.

## Little moments
- The inbox-zero pigeon naps (try poking it), flags flutter, Trash is swept away, counts pop with a feather when they hit zero, the pigeon pecks while loading and peers through a magnifier when search finds nothing, and wears a nightcap late at night.
- In Settings the pigeon dresses in your theme, bobs along to sounds, and reacts to text size. Tap it in General › Help a few times…
- Occasional milestone cards (never more than one a month).
- The appearance button shows a sun, a moon, or a half-lit disc for "match macOS", and the synced cloud hops in after a sync you start.
- Everything respects Reduce Motion.

## Fixed and improved
- Icons are crisper on standard-resolution screens.
- The Chime and Bowl sounds now fade out instead of stopping abruptly.
- The Dock badge leaves out accounts whose notifications are muted.
- Search fields reliably take the keyboard when they open.
