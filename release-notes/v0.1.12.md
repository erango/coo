## New
- **Message list styles:** Cards (each message in its own box, the new default), Day bands (one panel per day, two-line rows) or Classic. Settings › Appearance › Message list. **Sender avatars** add initials, or the sender's photo when Sender photos is on, to any style; on by default.
- **Search where you are:** every folder has its own search box (⌘F). A chip shows what's searched, "N more in other folders" offers to widen, and ⇧↵ or ⌥⌘F searches all mail. ⌘K offers "Search Inbox for…" first, then all mail. The sidebar's ⌘K pill is now "Go to or do…".
- **Add account:** one sheet for every provider. Type your address and Coo finds out whether it's Google, Microsoft 365 or iCloud, even for your own domain; or pick one yourself.
- **Actions on new-mail banners:** Archive, Delete, Mark as Read and Reply, right from the notification.
- **macOS Mail sound:** pick "macOS Mail" in Settings › Notifications › Sounds to hear Mail's own new-mail sound.
- **⌘A** in the message list selects every message in the folder.
- **Report a Bug…** in the Help menu, Settings › About and ⌘K: a short form that goes straight to the developer, with app details but never your mail.
- **iCloud send-from addresses:** Coo finds your @icloud.com addresses and lets you add aliases in Settings › Accounts, so you can send from them. An Apple ID at another provider (like Gmail) is no longer offered as a sender.

## Improved
- Accounts that share an address are labeled (for example "· iCloud" and "· Gmail") in every picker.
- Messages being sent show a progress bar, and rows slide in and out of the list with snappier motion.
- Mail whose Hebrew (or accented Latin) text arrived garbled, typically from older Outlook, is repaired when you open it.

## Fixed
- The About window no longer shows a copyright line.
