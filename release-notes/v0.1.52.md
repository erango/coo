## New
- A conversation's message rows in the list have their own read/unread button (on hover) and right-click menu, acting on that one message. Right-clicking a message of a conversation in the reading pane opens the same menu.

## Fixed
- In the composer, a Hebrew list (and what you type after it) stays on the right instead of jumping to the left of the window.
- A list item you align by hand keeps its number or bullet next to the text.
- Notices raised from a right-click menu (watching a conversation, making a sender a VIP, muting) now go away after a few seconds instead of staying on screen.
- The notice after "Watch this conversation" says what will happen: new replies will be listed under Needs you, in the sidebar.
