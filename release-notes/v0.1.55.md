## New
- Mail rules. Settings › Rules lets you say what should happen to mail from a sender, to an address, or with certain words in the subject: archive it, mark it read, move it to a folder or label, star it, delete it, keep it quiet, or put it in Needs you.
- Rules run in Gmail or Outlook when they can, so they keep working while Coo is closed. Anything the server can't do, Coo does as the mail arrives.
- Right-click a message for "Always archive mail from …" or "Make a rule for …". The rule editor shows what it would already catch, and can tidy up the matching mail in your inbox straight away.
- Rule activity shows what each rule did in the last 30 days, with Undo.
- Filters you made in Gmail or Outlook are listed too, and you can take one over to edit it in Coo.
- When you clear a sender's mail three times in a week without opening it, Coo offers to make a rule. You can turn this off.
- Muted senders are now rules. The ones you had move over by themselves.
- AI agents can add, list and remove rules (coo rule-add, coo rules, coo rule-remove, and the matching MCP tools). Adding and removing need writes turned on.

## Note
- Outlook accounts need one more Microsoft permission to save rules on the server. Settings › Rules shows an Allow button; until then their rules run in Coo.
