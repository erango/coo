## New
- **Drag messages to Finder:** drop a message (or a selection) on a folder or the desktop to save it as an .eml file, the original as sent. Dragging onto sidebar folders, labels and the message you're writing works as before.
- **Company logos:** senders without a Gravatar show their brand's own logo (BIMI), as Gmail does. Part of Settings › Privacy › Sender photos.
- **Show in Finder** on "Saved …" notices for attachments, images and messages.

## Improved
- Archiving in a quick burst keeps up: rows leave faster, and the next row's buttons are ready under the pointer right away.
- AI agents (Coo's MCP server) keep working after Coo updates, without restarting the agent; reading a conversation shows a preview for any message whose text can't be fetched instead of failing.

## Fixed
- Clearer error reports for sync problems.
