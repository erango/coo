## New
- **Empty Trash in Gmail:** Gmail accounts can now empty Trash and delete messages in Trash for good. Coo now asks Google for full access to Gmail; accounts you added before are asked once, in your browser, the first time you delete for good.
- **Select by dragging:** drag from empty space in the message list to select the messages a rectangle covers (hold ⌘ or ⇧ to add to what's selected). The list scrolls when you drag to its edge.

## Changed
- In light mode, unread messages now stand out: they're white, and read ones sit on a tinted background (it was the other way round).
