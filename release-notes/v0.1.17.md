## New
- **Drag to folders:** drag a message, or a selection of messages, onto a folder or label in the sidebar to move it there. Closed groups and collapsed accounts open as you hover. Undo puts them back.
- **Save attachments:** right-click an attachment for Open, Save As… and Save All Attachments…. Messages with two or more attachments get a "Save all…" link above them.
- **Calendar checkboxes:** uncheck a calendar in the calendar sidebar to hide its events. Coo remembers the choice, and hidden calendars also stay out of Next up and reminders.

## Improved
- **Focus and Do Not Disturb:** new-mail and meeting sounds now play through the macOS notification, so Focus and Do Not Disturb silence them.
- **Calendar colors:** each calendar gets its own color from the theme, including several calendars in one account. Accounts with the same address at two providers get different colors.
- **Avatars:** a person's avatar color comes from their address, so it's the same whichever account they wrote to.
