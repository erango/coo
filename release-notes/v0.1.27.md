## A clearer message view
- **Each message is its own block:** who wrote it, to whom and when sit in a header row with Reply and More, and the email's own words are set apart below, so it's clear what's Coo and what's the email.
- **Quoted history is tidy:** earlier messages fold behind "Quoted: N earlier messages". Open it and each one shows who wrote it and when, as stacked cards, or as a soft rail if you prefer (Settings › Reading › Quoted text).
- **Sender's colors** are now a small droplet button in the toolbar instead of a button inside the email.
- **Replying:** the message fades into a floating reply card with your avatar; Reply, Reply all and Forward show your shortcut keys.
- The toolbar casts a soft shadow once you scroll a message.

## Also new
- AI agents can add a Google Meet or Zoom call to events they create.
- Search is a quiet button in the list header; the folder's name and account each get their own line, so neither is cut short.
- Mint's dark accent is a cooler cyan-mint.

## Fixed
- Classic list with sender photos: avatars no longer sit on top of names, and rows have even edges.
- Empty folders no longer flash "Fetching" on every background sync.
- The sidebar no longer has a gap above Inbox, and the appearance icon matches its neighbours.
