## New
- **Delete in Trash deletes for good** (Microsoft 365 and iCloud). Undo still works for a few seconds. Gmail empties its Trash by itself after 30 days.
- **Drag an attachment to Finder** (a folder or the desktop) to save it there.
- **Edit the quoted text** in replies and forwards: unfold it with "•••" and type.
- **Attachments on top:** a message's files now show right under the sender, before the text. Settings › General can put them back below.
- **Search suggests people:** type part of a name or address and pick a contact to search mail from them.

## Improved
- **Notices:** confirmations like "Archived" and "Deleted" now appear in the window's bottom-right corner instead of across the message list, so they no longer look like one more message. In light mode they're dark, so they stand out. They also show in Settings, and stay clear of the Send button while you're writing.

## Fixed
- Reply, Reply all and Forward keys (and the other single-key shortcuts) work with a Hebrew keyboard layout, and after clicking into a message.
- Hebrew text in a new message starts at the right edge on a wide window, not mid-window.
