## New
- **Threads | Messages switch in the list:** if you like flipping between conversations and single emails, turn on Settings › Appearance › Switch in the list and the switch sits above the message list again.
- **Reconnect your AI agent:** when an agent's connection to Coo is out of date (usually after an update), Coo shows step-by-step instructions for each agent you've set up.
- **Agents can send and delete saved drafts:** besides writing drafts, an agent can now send one you've approved, or throw one away.

## Improved
- **Account nicknames:** press Enter to finish naming an account.

## Fixed
- Drafts with no recipients yet now save to your mailbox's Drafts folder. Before, they stayed only in Coo.
- Drafts that AI agents write now show up in Gmail, Outlook and iCloud Drafts. Before, they were saved only inside Coo.
- Opening a conversation always opens its newest message, even when every message in it is already read.
- Recipient suggestions stopped working when your mail included a message with no sender.
