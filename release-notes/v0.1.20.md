## New
- **Paragraph menu** in the composer's formatting bar: alignment, direction (auto, right to left, left to right), line spacing, space between paragraphs and letter spacing.
- **Drafts open ready to edit.** Pick a draft in Drafts and it opens in the editor, in the reading pane or its own window per Settings › Composing. A conversation that ends with your unsent reply opens it inline, right where you left it.

## Improved
- Tighter default spacing between lines and paragraphs in new messages, and in what recipients see.
- Shift+Enter starts a new line within the same paragraph.

## Fixed
- A long draft or reply no longer pushes the Send button out of view.
