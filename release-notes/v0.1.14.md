## New
- **Tidier sidebar:** your own folders and Gmail labels sit in a Folders (or Labels) group under each account, closed by default. Hover one and click the pin to keep it outside the group. Coo remembers both choices.
- **iCloud+ custom domains:** an alias on your own domain is picked up from mail it received and offered in Send from, once Coo confirms the domain's mail is hosted by iCloud.

## Improved
- **Garbled Hebrew:** letters the garbling destroyed (א, ם, נ) are now chosen with macOS's Hebrew spell checker, so far fewer words come back wrong, and no stray spaces are left behind.
