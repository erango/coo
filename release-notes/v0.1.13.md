## Improved
- **Garbled Hebrew mail is repaired everywhere:** messages already on your Mac are fixed when you open them (0.1.12 only fixed newly downloaded ones), and letters the garbling dropped (א, ם) come back.
- **Cards look as designed:** even 6 px gaps, a softer edge, a lift on hover and an accent ring on the selected message. The list header and search box now sit on the same background as the cards.
- **Choosing a list style:** Settings › Appearance › Message list shows a preview of each style, and the previews follow the Sender avatars switch.
- **Avatars in Day bands** are smaller and centered; with avatars on, unread shows as a badge on the avatar instead of a second dot.
