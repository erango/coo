## New
- **"Draft" in the message list:** a conversation with a draft in it shows "Draft" after the names, whether the draft is in your Drafts folder or a reply you're writing in Coo.
- **Show quoted text unfolded:** Settings › Reading › Quoted text can open messages with the quoted history already expanded, for people who answer inline, between your lines.
- **Plain quotes:** a third look next to Cards and Rail shows a message as it was sent, one stretch of text you can select from the reply into the quote.
- **Format quoted text:** when you edit the quoted original in a reply, the whole toolbar works there too: bold, links, colors, lists and the rest.
- **Paragraph direction buttons:** Left to right and Right to left in the toolbar force the selected paragraphs' direction.

## Improved
- **Tidier toolbar:** formatting buttons sit in small groups (text, color, paragraph, insert) so they're easier to scan.
- **Closing a reply asks first:** the ✕ on a reply asks whether to save it to Drafts or discard it, instead of throwing it away.
- **Replies are kept when you move on:** leaving a conversation with a reply open saves it to Drafts.
- **Flags stand out:** a flagged message's flag is filled in, in the flag color, on the toolbar buttons too.
- **Fresh labels:** Label as… and Move to… load your current labels and folders as they open, so ones made or deleted on the web show right away.
- **Update ready:** the Update ready pill gives a gentle ripple now and then.
- **Day bands:** when the top or bottom message of a day is archived, the one taking its place rounds its corners smoothly.

## Fixed
- Text typed into the quote of some older Outlook messages was partly hidden behind the quote's background.
