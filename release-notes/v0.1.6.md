## New
- **Right-click a message** in the list for Reply, Reply all, Forward, Archive, Delete, Move, Flag and Mark read. With several selected, it acts on all of them.
- **Double-click a message** to open it in its own window, sized to your screen.
- **Forwards keep their attachments**, and each one shows as a chip you can remove before sending.
- **Make an inline reply bigger:** a new button grows it to fill the reading pane.
- **Coo reopens where you left off:** the folder you had open last time.
- **Settings has its own sidebar** while it's open, with a clear "Back to Mail" at the top (or press Esc, ⌘1 or ⌘2).
- **Connect an AI agent, redone:** pick your agent on the left and follow the steps on the right. Each agent shows whether it's connected, Claude Desktop connects in one click, and copy buttons confirm with "Copied".

## Fixed
- Clicking a suggested recipient adds them, instead of leaving what you typed (macOS autocorrect was undoing the pick).
- The To field of a forward is ready to type in, with a visible cursor.
- The "•••" in a reply or forward opens the quoted message.
- Attaching from this Mac lets you pick any file; files were all grayed out.
- The Google Drive picker shows which account you're using and fills the browser window.
- Message previews no longer end in a stray "…" when there's nothing more to show.
- If an agent can't use your sign-ins, the error says how to fix it.
