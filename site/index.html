Questions

Is it safe to install?

Yes. Coo is signed by its developer and checked by Apple (“notarized”), so your Mac opens it without warnings. Passwords and sign-ins are kept in your Mac's Keychain.

Does it cost anything?

No. Coo is free.

Which accounts work?

Microsoft 365 and Outlook.com, Gmail and Google Workspace, and iCloud. Most people connect a few: work, personal, maybe a family one.

How do updates work?

Automatically. Coo checks for new versions quietly and installs them the next time you quit, so you're always up to date.

What does Coo see of my mail?

Only your Mac sees it. Coo keeps a copy on your computer so it's fast, and talks directly to your email provider. Crash reports, if something goes wrong, are anonymous: addresses and message text are removed before they leave your Mac, and you can switch them off in Settings. Privacy details.

Can my AI assistant use it?

Yes. Coo is built to work with assistants like Claude. Open Coo, choose Help › Connect an AI Agent, and copy the one line it shows (or click “Add to Claude Code”). By default the assistant can only read and search; it can only send, archive or answer invites if you allow it, and even then replies start as drafts. Everything runs on your Mac. Developers: run coo agent-help.

How do I remove it?

In Coo, open Settings and sign out of your accounts, then drag Coo from Applications to the Trash.
